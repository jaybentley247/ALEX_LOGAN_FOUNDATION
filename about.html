Our Story

A legacy of compassion, built on action.

The Alexander Logan Foundation was established in honour of Alexander Logan — a man known in his community for quietly paying school fees, visiting the sick and opening his door to anyone in need. His family and friends founded the organisation to make sure that spirit of generosity would outlive him and reach far more people.

What began as a small community initiative supporting a handful of families has grown into a trusted non-profit serving thousands every year. We work hand-in-hand with local leaders, schools, health workers and volunteers to deliver programmes that are practical, transparent and built to last.

  • Registered non-profit with full financial transparency
  • Community-led programmes with measurable outcomes
  • Every donation tracked from giver to beneficiary

The Man Behind the Name

Who was Alexander Logan?

Alexander Logan was a teacher, a neighbour and, to many, a lifeline. For more than thirty years he quietly paid school fees for children whose parents could not, sat with the sick when no one else came, and kept his front door open to anyone who needed a meal or a word of encouragement.

He never sought recognition. Most of the families he helped only discovered the full extent of his generosity after he passed away, when dozens of former students — now nurses, engineers, teachers and business owners — came forward to share how he had changed the course of their lives.

Those stories became the seed of this foundation. His family and friends made a promise: that the helping hand Alexander offered to one person at a time would be extended to thousands.

Our Journey

Milestones along the way.

From a single act of remembrance to a movement spanning dozens of communities — every step forward has been made possible by donors, partners and volunteers who believe in our work.

  1. 2015

    The foundation is born

    Months after Alexander’s passing, his family gathered the former students he had supported and pooled their resources. The Alexander Logan Foundation was officially registered with a single goal: to keep 25 children in school who were at risk of dropping out.

    Run entirely by volunteers from a borrowed office, the team visited each family personally, paid outstanding fees and supplied uniforms and books for the year.

    • 25 children sponsored
    • 12 founding volunteers
  2. 2016

    Mentorship programme begins

    We quickly learned that paying fees was only half the battle. Many of our scholars needed guidance, encouragement and someone to believe in them — just as Alexander had done.

    The Logan Mentors programme paired every sponsored child with a trained volunteer who meets with them monthly, tracks their progress and supports their families.

    • 80 scholars enrolled
    • 40 trained mentors
  3. 2017

    First medical outreach

    Parents of our scholars kept raising the same concern: the nearest clinic was hours away. In partnership with volunteer doctors and nurses — two of them former Logan students — we held our first free medical outreach.

    Over three days the team provided health screenings, malaria tests, eye checks, antenatal care and medicines, and referred serious cases to partner hospitals at no cost to the patient.

    • 1,000+ patients seen
    • 3 rural communities
  4. 2019

    Clean water for schools

    Children were missing lessons to fetch water, and waterborne illness was keeping others at home. We launched our first water and sanitation project, drilling boreholes and building hand-washing stations at partner schools.

    Attendance at those schools rose noticeably within the first term, and each project is now maintained by a trained community water committee.

    • 6 boreholes drilled
    • 4,500 people with clean water
  5. 2020

    Emergency relief response

    When the pandemic struck, many of the families we served lost their daily income overnight. We paused non-essential activities and turned our entire network toward emergency relief.

    Volunteers packed and delivered food parcels, hygiene kits and face masks door-to-door, while our mentors kept in touch with scholars by phone so that no child lost hope or fell behind.

    • 3,800 families reached
    • 20,000+ relief packs delivered
  6. 2022

    Our first scholars graduate

    A proud moment: the first group of children sponsored in 2015 completed secondary school, and several earned places at university and technical colleges with continued foundation support.

    Many of them now volunteer as mentors themselves — carrying Alexander’s helping hand forward to the next generation.

    • 94% completion rate
    • 18 alumni now mentors
  7. 2023

    Empowerment hubs launched

    To tackle poverty at its root, we opened community empowerment hubs offering training in tailoring, catering, agriculture, hairdressing and digital skills.

    Graduates receive start-up kits and small-business grants, plus ongoing coaching. Women’s savings cooperatives formed at the hubs now help members save, borrow and grow together.

    • 650 trainees graduated
    • 210 businesses launched
  8. Today

    45+ communities and growing

    Ten years on, the foundation that began with 25 children now touches more than 12,500 lives across 45 communities, powered by a family of over 600 volunteers and a growing network of partners.

    Our next goal is ambitious: to double our scholarship programme, open three new empowerment hubs and bring regular healthcare to every community we serve by 2030.
