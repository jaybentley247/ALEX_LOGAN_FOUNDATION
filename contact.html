FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How is my donation used?

Your gift goes directly to our education, healthcare, empowerment and relief programmes. We publish annual reports so you can see exactly where funds are spent.

Can I choose which programme to support?

Yes. Mention your preferred programme in the message form and our team will make sure your donation is allocated accordingly.

How do I become a volunteer?

Select “Volunteering” in the form above and tell us a little about your skills and availability. We’ll get back to you with upcoming opportunities.

Can my company partner with the foundation?

Absolutely. We work with businesses on sponsorships, employee giving and community projects. Choose “Partnership / Sponsorship” in the form to start the conversation.
