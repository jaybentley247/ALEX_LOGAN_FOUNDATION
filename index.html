Who We Are

A legacy of compassion, built on action.

Founded in honour of Alexander Logan, our foundation was born from a simple belief: that a helping hand at the right moment can change the course of a life. Today we serve thousands of people every year through programmes that are practical, transparent and built to last.

  • Registered non-profit with full financial transparency
  • Community-led programmes with measurable outcomes
  • Every donation tracked from giver to beneficiary

What We Do

To uplift lives and build self-reliant communities.

We break cycles of poverty by investing in people — their minds, their health and their futures.

Education

Scholarships, school supplies and mentorship that help children dream bigger.

Healthcare

Medical outreach, maternal care and health education for strong families.

Community Empowerment

Skills training and small-business grants that create lasting livelihoods.

Relief & Welfare

Food, clean water and emergency support for families facing hardship.
